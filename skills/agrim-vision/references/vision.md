# Deep pass — Vision (L1: why this exists)

Loaded on demand by `agrim-vision` when the user wants to go deep on the Vision tier. Fills the
**## Vision** section of `.sdlc/context/north-star.md`. Horizon: long-term (revisit ~quarterly). Keep
it direction, not a spec. The user owns the words; you elicit, structure, and stress-test.

## Pre-flight (read before asking)
- The current **## Vision** section of `.sdlc/context/north-star.md` (refresh vs. blank page).
- The repo's **README**, top-level structure, and recent `git log` — for signals of what this is and
  who it serves. Draft a first pass from these; don't hand over a blank page.

## Elicit (one or two questions at a time, not a wall)
1. **The deep problem** — what human pain does this address, and why does it deserve a sustained
   product rather than a feature or a workaround?
2. **The core user** — who actually lives with the outcome? Name the real person, not a buyer or a
   persona.
3. **The world if we win** — a concrete 3–10 year outcome: what becomes possible, at what scale, that
   isn't today?
4. **Negative space** — what is this explicitly *not*? Adjacent problems, methods, or audiences you
   exclude on purpose.
5. **Why now, why us** — what changed in the world that makes this the moment, and what makes this
   effort the one to do it?

## Stress-test before you write
- Would you still build this if a competitor shipped it in six months?
- If users adopted everything and shrugged, what's missing from the vision?
- What are you assuming the reader already knows that isn't on the page?

## Write into `## Vision`
A few tight paragraphs: the problem · who we serve · the world if we win · what we are not · why
now/us. **Never overwrite a tier the user already filled** without asking. If this contradicts the
Strategy or Design tiers, flag the cascade so they can be reconciled.
