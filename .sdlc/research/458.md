## Readers a prune must not break
verify: `done_refusal` (record done, merge gate), `_test_first_refusal` (pr), `reanchor_content` -- all before the goal is done. landing: `work` merge gate (`cross_repo.recorded`). propagation: `feature_propagate.recorded` suppression only (loss = one repeated note). escalation: `escalate`, `--show`, phase dispatch ceiling (live goal only). run_stop: dedupe marker only.

## Decision per family
- verify, landing, propagation, escalation, unit-tracking: NEW bounded pruner for goals whose newest action-log internal row is `recorded result=done` (terminal), run at the end of `_record done` for that goal and as a bounded sweep for crash leftovers.
- phase, agents: already bounded on the clean path; sweep adds crash-leak cleanup (agents only when no marker is alive).
- work: intentionally retained while its worktree exists (`work.finish` is the lifecycle owner; deleting the record orphans a worktree with possibly uncommitted work). 6 of 16 on the real checkout belong to closed issues with surviving worktrees. Ceiling: 169 B per worktree; lever `work.py finish --force`.
- run_stop: age-based prune (30 days mtime); inert dedupe marker, worst case of pruning a live run's marker is one duplicate run_stop row.
- withheld: documented retained -- it can be the only full copy of a kit finding when upstream filing is unavailable; operator deletes after filing.
- goal-review plan/report: documented retained -- plan is the re-run input of `compile_plan.py`, keyed by story number never passed to `record`; no code-level terminal signal; gitignored, per confirmed design.
