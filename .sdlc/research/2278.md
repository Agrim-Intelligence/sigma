## Goal in one paragraph
Create `skills/sigma-loop/scripts/reuse.py` with one verb, `check-research <sdlc_dir> <goal> [--at-research]`,
that finds the goal's research dossier exactly the way the reviewer brief does (`_phase_doc`, then
`_branch_doc`), decides legacy vs new format (`is_new_format`: the Reuse section carries a `**Source:**`
or `**Searched at:**` line), and in the new format checks the Searched-at commit (40/64 hex; must exist
with `--at-research`, else a warning), the recorded queries (1-25, pattern 1-200 chars, path safety,
no control characters), the `none: <reason>` form, and the RU table (exact header, five cells, unique
`RU-<n>`, six decisions each with an evidence/reason rule). Writes nothing. Plus `tests/test_reuse.py`,
one card entry, one CHANGELOG bullet. Six later slices (#2279-#2285) extend the same file.
