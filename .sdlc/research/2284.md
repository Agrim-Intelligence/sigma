## Goal in one paragraph
When `review_context.brief()` inlines a research dossier for `plan-review`, `code-review` or `pr-review` and the dossier is in the new Reuse format (`reuse.is_new_format`), append the exact `reuse.py rerun` command: plain `rerun` for plan-review (with the FIX-FIRST rule for a contradicted `new` decision), `rerun ... --new-since-research` for code-review and pr-review. Dossier absent, legacy, or any other review kind: the brief stays byte-identical. The builder never runs `reuse.py`; it only names the command.
