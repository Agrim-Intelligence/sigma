## Findings

1. **`inbox.md` is unbounded between loop picks**, and `loop.py next` prints the whole file to stderr
   (into the agent's context). A watcher running with no loop adds about 50 KB per day at one item per
   15-minute tick. Real growth. A hold-at-cap was designed in the plan and withdrawn after plan review (unsound as a small change); recorded as a documented decision with a queued follow-up.
2. **`setup-wizard-dismissed.json` is bounded only by skill prose**: the writer accepts any name.
   Gets a one-line code bound (the reader already ignores names outside `_MODES`).
3. **Pruner containment is asserted nowhere.** Every pruner checks its own leaf for a symlink, but
   `work.prune_terminal_review_copies` checks only the last component of `.sdlc/evidence/<goal>`; a
   symlinked `.sdlc/evidence` would let it `rmtree` outside `.sdlc`. Confirmed by the control test, which also
   found `loop._prune_dead_session_entries` following a linked `state/sessions`; both fixed.
4. **`embeddings.json` bytes are unbounded in dimension.** Capped by entry count; the byte ceiling is
   operator-chosen. Out of scope for this slice (changing eviction can force paid re-embeds); a queued
   follow-up is filed.
5. **`review-queue.md` (owned by #459's row, not claimed here)** appends a heading and two or three lines per parked
   or failed goal and nothing ever trims it. It is the same shape as the unbounded cursors; reported to the
   #459 slot, not changed.
