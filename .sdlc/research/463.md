## What the existing pruners cover
- `work.prune_terminal_review_copies` (loop.py `_record`, only for `done` and `failed`): removes only a
  non-symlink direct `wt` of a non-symlink direct `rv*` under `evidence/<safe-goal>`; fail-open; logs a
  file/delete action. Tests exist in `tests/test_work.py` and a lifecycle control in `tests/test_loop.py`
  (plan 349 records them red). It does NOT cover: any other name (`me`, `fin`, `mutwt`, `mainsnap`), the
  rest of an `rv*` directory, loose files, a goal that ended before the pruner existed, a goal that was
  parked or whose record never ran, or a failed prune (nothing retries it).
- `kg.retain_web`: opt-in (`web_retention_enabled: true` or the `kg.py retain` verb), age 90 days or a byte
  bound derived from disk (`disk.total * 0.0001`), archive not delete, idempotent, `tests/test_kg_retention.py`.
  The archive it fills is never pruned.
- `kg.py maintain` is report-only for analysis notes (flags over 200 files, prints the archive command).
