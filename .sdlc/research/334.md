# #334 research

The capture seam is Python's standard audit interface.  A `sitecustomize.py` directory placed first
on `PYTHONPATH` is inherited by ordinary Python children and is skipped by `-I`, `-E`, and `-S`.
The runner therefore records launch intent for non-Python processes but cannot inspect their network
connections.  Evidence must only describe the local, scripted gestures and not infer activity from
unexecuted opt-in integrations.
