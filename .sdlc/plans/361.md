the 40M checkpoint, decided 2026-10-04), `.sdlc/acceptance/361.md`. Worktree `.sdlc/work/361`.

## Design

The spend already happened before this PR (five metered fresh-reviewer runs, plus an extra A06 review; cumulative
3,887,193 processed tokens, $2.50 against the $75 ceiling). This change records it and recomputes ceilings.

- `docs/launch/evidence/cost-calibration.md` (create): one section per pilot P-a to P-d, per-run tables (tokens in/out,
  cache read/write, processed, cache-read share, dollars, wall time, agent id), the control result, a spend-against-ceiling
  table, and what the numbers do not show. P-c and P-d are `Not measured:` with the reason and what the owner supplies.
- `docs/launch/review-plan.md` (modify): execution table gains a measured column; new "Measured ceilings" table, each
  row with its formula and the 1.3 headroom; the 40M/75M counters after the pilots; the pilot-counting owner decision
  recorded as made on 2026-10-04; a new flagged OWNER DECISION to approve the measured ceilings.
- `tests/test_review_plan.py` (modify): drop `cost-calibration.md` from the "still to produce" absence guard (it now
  exists); add tests that the record has a section per pilot, no host path, rows that sum to the cumulative tokens, a
  control that a wrong row is caught, formulas and counters in the plan, the decision recorded.

Honest limits stated in both pages: one run per unit, no seeds, medium-effort single pass, extrapolated verification
rate, second vendor not included, S1/S2/S5/S6/S8/S10 and S9 unmeasured.
