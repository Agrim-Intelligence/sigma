# Plan: #2109 — an instruction for a verify that holds mid-goal

Lane: small. Skill wording plus a pinning test; no `loop.py` change (the issue's first proposal).

## Facts read (this checkout, after merging main)

- `loop.py verify` holds with exit 5 and a stderr line starting `RESOURCE-HOLD exit=5 -- ... retry_after=<s>`
  (`loop.py`, the `guarded.hold` branch). It runs nothing and writes no evidence. Exit 5 is also `UNVERIFIED`.
- The skills instruct only the pick-time `HOLD` (`land.md` step 2, `sigma-loop/SKILL.md`, `picking.md`).
- `review_cycles` is written in one place, `work.py` post-review, on a `block` verdict only. A hold posts no verdict,
  so it cannot be counted; the instruction has to say not to turn one into a review.
- `resource_lease.py wait` takes `--kind verify|session|goal` (default `goal`) and `--max <s>`.
- `qkind.QKINDS` has no hold kind; `no_evidence` (could not measure, nothing proven) is the nearest honest one.
- `evals/phase_context_budget.json`: orchestrator and goal-slot sit within 4 and 1 estimated tokens of their
  ceilings, and a ceiling may not be raised. So the detail goes in a NEW reference outside the budget table; the
  budgeted files get a pointer that is paid for by trimming words in the same file.
