## Design
- The ceiling is a stated margin (at least 2x) over the max wall time read from the `test` job logs of real PR runs of this branch (re-run attempts of the same commit are separate measurements, as in #1047); count and max go in budget.json. Step 2's time is reported in the log and in budget.json as measured context; the ceiling covers the AC-6 subject, the extended control.
- Scale: serial, one control run costs the sum of its variants (about 2 minutes on a laptop); a new variant adds linearly, which is what the ceiling alarms on. Added to the required job: three control runs (budget step, two planted tests under `-n 2`); the added minutes are measured on CI before the ceiling is set, against a 45 minute timeout and a 24-28 minute baseline.
- Resilience: budget.py kills nothing and leaves no state; the control removes its own temp tree. Chokepoint named: the ceiling sits in the one required check, so a slow runner blocks PRs. The lever is re-running the job; if a ceiling is wrong, a PR that edits budget.json with new measurements is the documented repair; the margin is chosen over the max for that reason.
- No model spend, no secrets, no network.
