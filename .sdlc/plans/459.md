## Design
- New `work.prune_terminal_review_generations(sdlc_dir, goal=None, limit=10, goal_done=False)` (next to `prune_terminal_review_copies`).
  It indexes the store once: manifests (`review-manifests/*.json`, goal + generation_id), generation dirs (`evidence.json` goal), posts (`goal`).
  A generation has owners = {manifest goals naming it} + {evidence goal}. Deletable for goal G only when owners == {G} (a successor owner's manifest
  naming it, or conflicting evidence, spares it). Evidence-less, unreferenced generations have no attributable owner and are never touched (documented residual).
- A goal G is terminal iff there is NO `state/work/<G>.json` (work.finish unlinks it, so the gate can no longer bind) AND (`goal_done` hint from `loop._record`,
  or the action log's last `claimed|recorded` entry for G is `recorded` with `result: done`). Parked, failed, running, re-claimed, unlogged: not terminal.
- Order per terminal goal (every step idempotent, fail-open, no step needs a file a previous step removed): its posts -> each owned generation's result ->
  generation dir (`shutil.rmtree`, non-symlink direct child with a valid id) -> its manifests, only when the generation they name is gone. A crash anywhere
  leaves a state the next call finishes; manifest-last keeps the goal attributable.
- Bounded: at most `limit` terminal goals pruned per call; candidates are goals present in the index, so cost is O(residual store), and terminal goals vanish from it.
- Wiring: `loop._record`, `outcome == "done"` branch, after `_release_checkout` (so the work record is already gone), with `goal_done=True`, fail-open try/except.
  Self-heal: the same call also sweeps other goals proven terminal by the log, so a crash between `record done` and the prune is repaired by the next `done`.
  Operator lever: `work.py prune-review-generations <sdlc_dir> [<goal> [--done]] [--limit N]` (polite: same bound, no process pausing).
- `review-queue.md`: documented intentionally unbounded (a human work queue; an entry is a decision awaiting a person; ceiling per parked/failed goal, reclaim by the operator who triages it).
- `docs/launch/dispositions/459.json` (11 entries, text copied from the scan, no `unscanned`) and `docs/launch/retention-review-evidence.md`
  (ownership, who re-reads what and for how long, measurements with commands and what was not measured, 10x/100x, residuals, recovery).
- Not changed: `review_context.py` writer (no supersession-time delete: a block-cycle fix republishes while the earlier generation's post may still need reconcile).

## Scale / recovery
Per review about 2 KB-92 KB (median 9 KB brief) + ~1.2 KB of manifest/post/result bytes; ~2.1 generations and ~37 KB per goal. Pruned goals cost 0 after done; residual = non-done goals + evidence-less generations.
Crash, partial prune and restore-from-backup: each call recomputes from the index and the log; a restored store is drained 10 goals per `done`.
